02 — Ceramic coating

Water hates it. That's the point.

Coated panel Live sim Hover = air blower

A ceramic coating is a liquid layer that bonds to your clear coat and cures into a hard, slick shell. Water can't get a grip on it, so it pulls into tight beads and rolls away, taking loose dirt with it.

01

Pre-rinse

Loose grit comes off first. Scrubbing grit into paint is how swirl marks are born.

Pressure washer · wide fan tip

02

Foam bath

Thick snow foam sits and softens the grime, so it slides off instead of scratching on the way out.

pH-neutral foam · foam cannon

03

Hand wash

Two buckets, grit guards, clean mitts. Top down, straight lines, never circles.

Two-bucket method · plush mitts

04

Decon

Iron remover and clay pull out what a wash can't. That sandpaper feel on the paint? Gone.

Iron fallout remover · clay

05

Polish

A machine polish lifts light swirls and haze. This is the step where the gloss comes back.

Dual-action polisher · finishing pads

06

Protect

Sealant or ceramic coating locks the finish in. Water beads, dirt lets go, you smile at reflections.

Sealant · ceramic coating
