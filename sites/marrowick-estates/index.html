The process

From plan
to home

  1. 01

    Plan

    We start where the architect did: the drawing. How rooms connect, where the light falls, what the site allows.

  2. 02

    Structure

    Walls rise, slabs settle, the roof floats clear. Good structure goes unnoticed — you feel it as calm.

  3. 03

    Light

    Glass only where it earns its place. As evening comes in, the rooms warm and the garden goes quiet.

  4. 04

    Living

    Then it is simply a home. Pool lit, trees grown in, nothing left to add.

The agency

We take on a handful of houses at a time, and get to know each one before you do.

Marrowick is a small agency with an architect's habits. We walk every house before it is listed, measure what matters, and describe how it is built — not only how many rooms it has.

Viewings are private and unhurried, one party at a time. If a house isn't right for you, we'll say so.
