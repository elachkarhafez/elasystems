§ 01 — House rules

Our house rules

We don't do “just send it.” We draw it, argue with it, redraw it — and only then put it on you. Forever is a long time to live with a rushed line.

I

One chair, full attention.

No conveyor belt. When you're booked, you're the only thing on the table. Literally.

II

Your idea, our hands.

Bring the napkin sketch, the screenshot, the half-remembered dream. We'll bring clean lines.

III

We'll tell you no.

Tiny script on a finger? Let's talk. Sometimes “no” is the best design advice you'll ever get.

        § 06 — Aftercare
        
Aftercare, Briefly

        
General pointers. Your artist gives you the real instructions for your piece — follow those.

      

      

        
Day one
Leave it be.
Keep the wrap on as long as your artist says. Clean hands before touching it. Yes, every time.

        
First weeks
Gentle & thin.
Mild wash, pat dry, a thin layer of whatever your artist recommends. Less is more.

        
Hands off
Don't pick.
It will itch. Don't scratch, peel or pick. It flakes on its own schedule, not yours.

        
Skip it
No soaking.
Pools, baths, the sea and sunbathing can wait until it's healed. The ocean isn't going anywhere.

        
Forever
Sunscreen.
Sun fades ink faster than regret. Once it's healed, cover it up or lotion it up.

      

      
Not medical advice. If something looks or feels wrong while healing, talk to a medical professional — then tell us, too.
